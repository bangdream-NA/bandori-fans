# Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct is adapted from [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) version 2.1, in a condensed form that follows the same spirit but reads more like a friendly fan community.

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

**Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:**

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community

**Examples of unacceptable behavior include:**

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces. Examples of representing our community include using an official e-mail address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Reporting and Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately via this repository's **[Private Vulnerability Reporting](../../security/advisories/new)** (Security tab → "Report a vulnerability"), and note in your description that this is a Code-of-Conduct report rather than a security vulnerability — GitHub currently has no dedicated private form for Code-of-Conduct reports, so we repurpose this existing private submission channel. Reports are visible only to the project maintenance team. We will review every report carefully and promptly, and will respect the reporter's privacy.

## Enforcement Guidelines

| Level | Situation | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Correction | Use of inappropriate language or other unprofessional or unwelcome behavior | A private, written warning clarifying the nature of the violation; a public apology may be requested |
| 2. Warning | A single incident or a series of violations | A formal warning with conditions on continued behavior; no interaction with the people involved for a specified period, with escalation possible for repeat violations |
| 3. Temporary Ban | A serious or sustained violation | A temporary ban from all community interaction and public communication for a specified period, with no contact of any kind with the people involved during that period; escalation to a permanent ban is possible for repeat violations |
| 4. Permanent Ban | A sustained pattern of violations, or harassment of an individual or group | A permanent ban from all public community interaction |
