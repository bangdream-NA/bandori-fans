bandori.fans is a fan database created for the BanG Dream! franchise (バンドリ). Whether you want to look up the full setlist of a particular live show, follow the release schedule for a CD, see whether a voice actor has a birthday today, or discover what happened “on this day” in the franchise’s history, you can find it all here. Everything is interconnected, so you can follow links and freely explore the entire web of information.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **10 bands, one-click immersion** — Select any band (POPPIN’PARTY, Roselia, MyGO!!!!!, Ave Mujica, and more), and the entire website adopts that band’s signature colors. The home page, news, and calendar are all filtered so you see only the band you care about.
- **A real-time subscribable calendar** — Add live shows, events, and CD releases to your preferred Google, Apple, or Outlook calendar with one click. It updates automatically, so you never miss a ticket sale or release.
- **A fully interconnected database** — Songs ⇄ bands ⇄ characters ⇄ voice actors ⇄ CDs ⇄ live shows ⇄ venues. Every entry leads to related entries, letting you explore continuously through linked information.
- **Three languages + light and dark themes** — Switch freely between 简体, 日本語, and EN, and choose light, dark, or system theme. Your choices are remembered.
- **Everyone can contribute** — Any visitor who spots an error can submit a correction online. The site is built entirely by fan volunteers and is both nonprofit and unofficial.

## 🏠 Home

When you open the home page, you will see:

- **Live status bar**: A row at the very top of the page with a pulsing dot and a “LIVE” label, showing when the data was last updated (for example, “Data version v 2026-06-24 15:34 JST”). Language and theme controls appear on the right.
- **Site header**: A large “BANDORi.FANS” logo, a short site introduction, and a **site-wide search box** (also available with the `Ctrl K` / `⌘ K` shortcut).
- **Band color selector**: A horizontal row containing “All Bands” and 10 bands, each preceded by a dot in its signature color. Selecting one applies its colors across the site and filters the content; your selection is remembered.
- **Today’s anniversary card** (// TODAY IN BANDORI): Today’s date plus commemorative events that happened “on this day,” such as voice actor birthdays and anniversaries of album releases or events. Each row includes the year and a band tag.
- **Next live Hero** (NEXT LIVE): A large color-block card in the relevant band’s theme, displaying the title, date, venue, and region of the next live show, with a one-click “View live details →” link.
- **Upcoming calendar preview** (UPCOMING): A horizontal date strip showing roughly the next two weeks. Today is highlighted, while live shows and releases appear as small colored blocks.
- **More section cards**: Four shortcut cards for the latest news, upcoming live shows, releases, and database index, each with a live item count and a direct link.

## 📰 News

Follow official and community news from across the BanG Dream! franchise in one place.

- **Featured news banner**: Three selected stories displayed as large cards with images, band tags, titles, and dates.
- **Multi-dimensional filters (combinable)**: Filter by **source** (Official / Bushiroad / Twitter / Other), **time** (within 7 / 30 / 90 days), **type** (Music / Media / BanG Dream! TV / Live Information / Merchandise / Playlists / In-person Events / Ticketing, and more), and **band**. When any filter is active, use “Reset filters ↺” to clear them all at once.
- **News list**: Each row displays the date, type, band, title (sometimes with a summary), and source badge, with pagination support.
- **News details**: Full text or summary, publication and update dates, source, related links, and related schedule entries (with links to the corresponding calendar events). At the bottom, “View original source ↗” opens the official website or post, followed by recommendations for other news about the same band.

## 📅 Calendar and Subscriptions

See all live shows, events, and releases on one page, and sync them to your own calendar.

- **Calendar subscriptions**: Provides a subscription URL (with one-click “Copy”) and three one-click buttons: “Google Calendar ↗,” “Apple Calendar ↗,” and “Outlook ↗,” together with step-by-step setup instructions. Subscriptions update automatically. When filtering by band, the feed becomes band-specific (for example, “Subscribe to the Roselia calendar”).
- **Three views**: Calendar, ticketing (an upcoming ticket-sales schedule), and list (an upcoming-events schedule).
- **Category filters**: All / Live Shows / Events / Releases / Ticket Sales / Talks / Fan Meetings / Exhibitions / In-store Events / Pop-up Stores / Collaboration Cafés, with a distinct color for each category.
- **Month navigation**: Previous month / Today / Next month plus year and month selectors. Your band, category, and view selections are preserved while navigating.
- **Month view (desktop)**: A seven-column weekly grid with today highlighted. When a day has too many events, “+N items” expands the rest. Canceled events are labeled and struck through. A “Ticketing Schedule” panel above the calendar also lists each ticket-sales window for the month.
- **Mobile**: Switches to a vertical “Schedule” list showing only dates with events, with a separate “This Month’s Ticketing Windows” section.

## 🎂 Today’s Anniversaries

A dedicated “What Day Is Today?” page with more detail than the home-page card. A date card (in Tokyo time) appears at the top, followed by five fixed “On This Day” categories:

- **Band anniversaries ★** — Bands that debuted or formed today (with an “Nth anniversary” label)
- **Voice actor birthdays ☆** — Voice actors celebrating a birthday today (ages are not shown for privacy)
- **Record release anniversaries ◇** — Records released on this date
- **Live show anniversaries ●** — Live shows held on this date in the past
- **News anniversaries ▣** — News published on this date in the past

Every item includes a year, band tag, and clickable title linking directly to its details. If all five categories are empty today, the page instead shows a “Tomorrow Preview” and a “Past 7 Days” recap.

## 🗂️ Database

The “Database” index brings together **six interconnected sub-databases**, each represented by a card with a live item count. Every list page supports pagination, ascending or descending sorting, band filters, and “Clear filters.”

### 🎤 Live Shows and Events
- **List**: Displays each show’s date, title (with a portrait poster thumbnail), band, venue, and statistics (number of performances, songs, and performers). Filter by “Upcoming / Ended” and by band. Canceled shows carry a struck-through badge. Each performance date of a multi-day tour occupies its own row.
- **Details**: A large landscape key visual plus date, venue, and official page; **Tracklist / SETLIST** (each song numbered and marked with the performing band’s color label, linked to its song page, with multi-date tours grouped by performance); **related news**; **performers** (performing / co-performing / singing tracks / related); and, for multi-venue tours, **tour venues**.

### 🎵 Songs
- **List**: Title (with romanization), band (signature-color tag), release date, included on (linked to the CD), catalog number, and counts for performances and tracks.
- **Details**: Song title and romanization, **alternate song titles** (aliases, localized translations, and multilingual titles), release date, duration, catalog number, and performers; cross-links to the band, **CD releases**, and **live performances**.

### 🎸 Bands
- **List**: 10 band cards (logo / name / romanization plus member, song, and performance counts), with an “Active / Disbanded” filter.
- **Details**: A full page in the band’s colors, with a Hero displaying its logo and information including debut date, member count, status, aliases, and series. Bands with multiple lineup eras have a “Lineup Eras” accordion. Cross-linked sections list members, songs, live shows, news, characters, CD works, and live setlists.

### 👤 Characters
- **List**: Character avatar cards (character name / band / CV voice actor), filterable by band.
- **Details**: Character-art Hero and name reading; clickable band and voice actor tags. Band members also include role (instrumental position), birthday, zodiac sign, blood type, height, measurements, representative color, and more; **voice actor history**, **performed songs**, a year-collapsible **appearance history**, and social-media accounts.

### 🎙️ Voice Actors
- **List**: First presents an “Explore by Band” overview (showing the number of voice actors for each band), then displays photos, names, readings, and CV character names after drilling down.
- **Details**: Voice actor photo Hero, name reading, and birthday (linked to the corresponding date on “Today’s Anniversaries”); social-media accounts and **portrayed characters** (character name / band / tenure, with active roles labeled).

### 📍 Venues
- **List**: Name, country/region, city, and statistics (event and performance counts). Locations not yet reviewed carry an “Unreviewed” badge. Filterable by country/region.
- **Details**: Country/region, city, address, and **upcoming live shows** with clickable links.

### 💿 CDs / Record Releases
- **List**: Each row displays the release date, band (signature-color tag), catalog number, record title (with cover thumbnail), type, and status, with “N tracks” and “N editions” badges. Type (album / single / compilation), band, and year filters can be combined; their values are stored in the URL so the filtered view can be bookmarked or shared. Undated releases display their official scheduling text (for example, “Scheduled for release in fall 2026”) instead of a fabricated date.
- **Details**: Large cover art, title, type badge, and catalog number; release date (linked to that day in the calendar) and participating bands. If applicable, a “First-press Limited Bonus” section is shown. The central feature is the **edition accordion** — each edition includes its catalog number, format (for example, “Includes Blu-ray”), price, and “Official page ↗.” Expanding it reveals tracks grouped by disc.

> These six major sub-databases are fully interconnected: songs ⇄ bands ⇄ characters ⇄ voice actors ⇄ CDs ⇄ live shows ⇄ venues. Starting from any entry, you can freely explore by following the links.

## 🔍 Site-wide Search & Random Exploration

- **Site-wide search**: Fuzzy matching across multiple categories (bands, songs, voice actors, characters, events, venues, records, and members), with results grouped by category. Open it from any page with `Ctrl K` / `⌘ K`.
- **Random entry ↗**: Jump to a random database detail page with one click — ideal for casual browsing and discovering something new.

## 🤝 Contribute / Submit Corrections

Found an error? Any visitor can submit a correction online.

- **Correction form**: Opening it from an entry’s “Correction” link automatically includes the reported item, or you can paste the page URL manually. Select the “Issue type” and “Affected field,” enter the required “Correct information,” and optionally provide a source URL, additional notes, and contact details.
- **More links**: The footer also includes an **Entry Editing Guide** (avoid spoilers, cite official sources, remain noncommercial, respect privacy, and stay neutral), **Contact Us**, and the **Changelog**.

---

## 🌐 Languages and Light/Dark Themes

- **Three-language switching**: Use “简体 | 日本語 | EN” in the top navigation. Switching languages **preserves your current page and filter settings** instead of returning you to the home page.
- **Light/dark themes**: The theme button cycles through **Light → Dark → Follow System** (☀ / ☾ / ⌬), and your choice is remembered.
